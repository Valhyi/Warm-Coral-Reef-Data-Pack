# Warm-Coral-Reef-Data-Pack

Makes warm oceans always full of coral reefs.

This mod adds a datapack that completely transforms seabed generation in the warm ocean biome. Unlike vanilla Minecraft generation, where corals only spawn in isolated patches, this modification removes restrictions to fill the seafloor with dense, colorful, and continuous coral reefs. It is ideal for players looking to explore a much more vibrant underwater world without altering the rest of the game.
